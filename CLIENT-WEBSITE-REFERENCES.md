# Client website reference playbook

User-selected references, reviewed 23 September 2026:

- Editorial identity: https://rar13h.github.io/socially-curated/mockup-a-editorial.html
- Complete visitor journey: https://rar13h.github.io/socially-curated/local-15.html

Use these as references when relevant to later client work in this workspace. Read the actual client brief first. A reference supplies principles, not the client's brand or facts.

## Reusable principles

1. Establish a clear visual identity with typography, image selection and consistent surfaces.
2. State the actual offer in the opening. Motion must leave the headline and primary action legible and usable.
3. Show a small amount of convincing work with context before asking for commitment.
4. Group services around a visitor's need; reveal details when requested.
5. Explain the next steps and the result of each step.
6. Add human or place-based evidence from approved client assets.
7. End with a useful, working action appropriate to the business.
8. Carry context into that action: a selected service should prefill an inquiry; a selected item should open the right order page.
9. Keep claims and image provenance honest. Do not present concepts, stock imagery, simulated feeds or generated people as client proof.
10. Make navigation, disclosures, forms and motion controls work with a keyboard and touch. Test the whole route, including failure states.

## Translate the journey to the business

| Client | Opening action | Useful evidence | Supporting content | Final action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Film / creative studio | Explore work | Real projects with role and deliverables | Services, process, people, scope questions | Inquiry or brief tied to selected service |
| Coffee shop | View menu / order | Drinks, food, space and actual location | Menu, hours, location, accessibility, coffee story | Working order link or directions |
| Event service | Check services | Relevant event coverage | Packages, availability, process, team | Date and event-type inquiry |
| Local appointment business | Choose service | Results and verified qualifications | Scope, prices if approved, location, policies | Actual appointment booking |

For coffee, translate the cinematic film rhythm into the sequence of making, pouring and serving. A video or code animation is a treatment, not the task: menu, prices, hours and directions must remain easy to find. Use local service content rather than importing an agency's package/portfolio structure.

## Questions that change a design

- Who is arriving, and what are they trying to do?
- What is the single most valuable action on a phone?
- Which approved assets and facts make the business credible?
- Which content earns a section, and which repeats something already answered?
- Where does the final action lead, and how are success and failure confirmed?

## End-to-end review

Read every label and claim. Walk from the first screen to the final action. Check narrow phones, landscape, tablet and desktop; keyboard focus; missing graphics; reduced motion; loading and form errors. Record actual devices and browsers used, and distinguish implemented support from verified support.
